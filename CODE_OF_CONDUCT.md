# Code of Conduct

EvoTrader follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep
discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing
someone's private information are not tolerated — and in a project about
trading, that includes anyone's account details, balances or positions.

The Covenant applies in every project space — issues, pull requests,
discussions — and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **adamc@immersialabs.com**. Reports
are read by the maintainer, kept confidential, and answered as quickly as
possible. The maintainer decides on consequences following the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines, from a private warning to a permanent ban.
